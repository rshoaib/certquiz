## 🎤 Brand Voice

- **Tone**: Direct, confident, anti-paywall. "Stop paying for X — do it free here."
- **Style**: Short sentences (<25 words). Bold key takeaways. Tables for comparisons.
- **Address**: Second person ("you"). Empathetic to exam stress, never condescending.
- **Differentiator**: Always mention "free, no signup, 100% private" within the first 100 words.
- **Competitor framing**: Reference paid tools (VCE Simulator $40, Kaplan, Boson) as the expensive alternative, position CertQuiz as the free option.
