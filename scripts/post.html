Focus on Performance-Based Questions (PBQs) First

The CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 exam introduces several simulation-style PBQs right at the start of your test. If you aren't prepared, these will steal your time and confidence. Practice configuring firewalls, setting up enterprise wireless networks, and matching attacks to mitigation strategies using hands-on labs.

Master the Acronyms

You cannot survive this exam if you don't know the difference between RTO, RPO, MTTR, and MTBF. We recommend reviewing the official CompTIA exam objectives PDF and creating flashcards for every acronym listed at the end of the document.
